HOW TO READ THIS Epstein Level

Each person has an Epstein level.

Level 0 is for Epstein's closest associates, who had extensive correspondence with him.

Level 1 is for people who went to the Virgin Islands or Zorro Ranch to interact with Epstein, or were in the black book or flight logs.

Level 2 is for people who directly mutually interacted with Epstein.

Level 3 is for people who were funded by an Epstein funded organization or conference.

Level 4 is for people who were mentioned.

Level 5 is for people who have plausible deniability - the person in the files may refer to someone else who is unrelated to any universities.

Level 6 is for people who share a name with someone who is mentioned in the files, but the name provably references someone who isn't that particular professor.

X is for people who seem to have actively rejected meeting with Epstein.
HOW TO READ THIS University Score

Each university is given an Epstein score:

100 Epstein Points for each level 0 or 1 person

10 Epstein Points for each level 2 person

1 Epstein Point for each level 3 person.

Each University also has an Epstein level: it's the lowest Epstein level of the people it has.

WHAT

Executive Summary

This page documents people who are both meaningfully connected to Epstein *and* who have some University affiliation - whether they are professors, administrators, or trustees.

By focusing on a narrow scope, the major contributors were able to provide the information they believed would produce the most meaningful impact.

The major contributors avoided research they viewed as superfluous to the mission.

At least one major contributor used AI to assist their work. At least one major contributor made a point of reaching out to people of most political leanings. No major contributor speaks any language other than English fluently.

No major contributor was financially compensated for this work. No major contributor expects any financial compensation, of any sort, as a result of this work.

WHY

Mission Statement

The people who Epstein funded and interacted with frequently share his beliefs, ideologies, and predilections. Among these are university professors - who often hold direct power over hundreds or thousands of young adults every semester, and extreme power over their graduate students for roughly six years.

The culture these people create is not one where people are treated fairly. It is one of favoritism and nepotism. When a university hires such a person, it both reflects on that university's culture and bolsters it.

This resource is for prospective college students.

Even if the relevant professors are gone, their culture remains. You should avoid going to a university that has Epstein's culture.

You are capable of going somewhere else, and you deserve a proper, safe, and healthy learning environment.
